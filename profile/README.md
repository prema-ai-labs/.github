### 🚀 About Prema AI Labs
**Prema AI Labs** is an Indian AI research and educational technology venture founded at KGiSL Institute of Technology (KG KITE) in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We engineer domain-specialized study companions aligned with university curricula and author open-source runtime tooling for autonomous multi-agent developer workflows.

* 🎓 **Flagship Platform:** [Prema AI Study Companion](https://premaailabs.tech/) — Free AI tutor engineered for Anna University engineering syllabi (Part-A 2-mark definitions, 16-mark structured diagrams, and Tanglish explanation engines with institutional Moodle integration).
* 🤖 **Core Agent Infrastructure:** [Gemini Antigravity Bridge](https://github.com/prema-ai-labs/gemini-antigravity-bridge) & 30-Agent Multi-Cursor Swarm Orchestrator running on consumer hardware.
* 🛡️ **Zero-Trust Security:** [AgentShield](https://github.com/prema-ai-labs/agentshield) — AST guardrails and policy enforcement for autonomous MCP tool execution ([Zenodo Preprint DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22259022](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22259022)).
